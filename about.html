About the concept

Creativity with a personal touch

UrbanStyle started with a simple idea: clothing and design can be ways to express individuality.

Our mission

To make personal styling and design ideas approachable, creative and easy to discuss.

Our vision

To develop a recognisable lifestyle brand known for creative thinking and useful customer guidance.

Our values

Creativity, respect, honesty, clear communication and quality.

How enquiries work

Visitors describe what they are looking for. The business must confirm a quote and availability before any booking.

Frequently asked questions

Can I book directly online?

No. You can submit an enquiry, but it does not create a confirmed booking.

Are prices fixed?

No prices are supplied in the original proposal. Costs depend on the type of service and must be quoted individually.

Where is UrbanStyle located?

The original website refers to Johannesburg, but it does not provide a verified shop address.
